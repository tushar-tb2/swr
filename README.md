# Profile

I am **Tushar N. Bhatt**, currently conducting Space Weather Research (SWR) data analysis for my PhD. I am a passionate researcher and continuous learner, constantly exploring new domains ranging from space physics and material science to software development.

## Academic Research: Solar Physics & Space Weather Research

My doctoral research focuses on understanding how our neighboring planets behave compared to Earth when influenced by solar transients, such as coronal mass ejections (CMEs) and corotating interaction regions. This study satisfies our curiosity about Earth's past, present, and future in space, which is critical for human survival and the protection of satellite communications.

* **Methodology:** I utilize various spacecraft observations alongside Community Coordinated Modeling Center (CCMC) models organized by NASA (USA).

I am also guided by my first supervisor, Prof. Rajmal D. Jain (Retired PRL Scientist, PI of SOXS Mission & RAS Fellow).

## Material Science & R&D Experience

As a Junior Research Fellow at the **FCIPT-Institute for Plasma Research** (an autonomous unit of the Dept. of Atomic Energy, India), I gained extensive R&D experience in Material Science involving experimental plasma (Argon, Nitrogen, Xenon). Under the supervision of Dr. Mukesh Ranjan, Dr. Mukul Bhatnagar, and Dean Prof. Subroto Mukherjee, my work included:

* **PVD & Sputtering:** Nanoparticle development via Physical Vapor Deposition (Magnetron Sputtering) of silver for solar cells, physical vapor deposition of carbon, and ion beam sputtering for ceramics.
* **Testing & Characterization:** Hands-on experience with FE-HRSEM and Energy Dispersive Spectroscopy (EDS), X-ray Diffraction (XRD), Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM), Contact Angle Measurement, FTIR, and Raman Spectroscopy. Conducted Magnetic Thruster Hysteresis testing.
* **Instrumentation Maintenance:** Maintained diffusion pumps and magnetrons, and conducted vacuum chamber leak tests.
* **Publication:** Our research is published and available at [AIP Scitation](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://aip.scitation.org/doi/10.1063/1.4964312).
