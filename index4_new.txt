        <div class="tile">
            <h2>Sunspot numbers & High Speed Streams (HSS)</h2>
            <ul>
                <li>Major <a href="http://sidc.oma.be/silso/datafiles" target="_blank">sunspot numbers</a> resulted in <a href="https://www.spaceweatherlive.com/en/help/what-is-a-coronal-hole.html" target="_blank">coronal holes</a> .</li>
                <li><a href="http://www.geodin.ro/varsiti/" target="_blank">HSS</a> often associated with coronal holes.</li>
                <li>The coronal holes can be traced by <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.11476" target="_blank">CHIMERA segmentation</a> available on <a href="https://www.solarmonitor.org/chimera.php" target="_blank">solar monitor</a> website.</li>
                <li>The solar wind speed due to coronal holes from poles is approx. 500-800km/sec while from nearby equator ranges from 300-400km/sec.</li>
                <li>The combination of these winds gives compressions of slow speed winds (300-400km/s) due to high speed winds (HSS) (500-800km/s).</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
