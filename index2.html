Sunspot numbers & High Speed Streams (HSS)

  • Major sunspot numbers resulted in coronal holes .
  • HSS often associated with coronal holes.
  • The coronal holes can be traced by CHIMERA segmentation available on solar monitor website.
  • The solar wind speed due to coronal holes from poles is approx. 500-800km/sec while from nearby equator ranges from 300-400km/sec.
  • The combination of these winds gives compressions of slow speed winds (300-400km/s) due to high speed winds (HSS) (500-800km/s).
